Flight view metrics

Click any ad on the monthly report to open its flight view—the grid with one column per day.

Breadth

How many media were active on a given day—the count of channels, placements, and linked live events (such as UND hockey) that ran that day. The teal Media line at the top of the flight view charts this day by day.

Depth

Depth is the times-seen ratio: how often an account in a medium’s audience is estimated to have been exposed to the ad (saturation), not raw impressions.

The × numbers in the flight grid are depth at a finer grain—not a raw impression count.

Where each × appears

  • Day cells — times seen on that calendar day. On digital/social lines, hover for the 7-day running average. Radio shows that day’s × only.
  • 7-day column — sparkline of daily × for digital/social (not radio). No end number on the spark—Lifetime holds the flight total; hover a day cell for the numeric 7-day average.
  • Lifetime column — times seen across the whole flight for this campaign (from first day on air to last), not lifetime in the abstract.

Reach in the Lifetime column is how many accounts (unique viewers or profiles) were reached in that medium—not raw impressions. Radio uses spots in that column instead of account counts; direct mail uses households. Some lines are tagged Sample (Meta and YouTube windows built from flight totals) or Estimated (radio listeners and AET account stand-ins until platform or rate-card numbers replace them).
